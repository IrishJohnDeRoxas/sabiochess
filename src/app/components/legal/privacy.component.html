TERMS OF SERVICE
01

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver high-performance chess analysis:

  • Local Preferences: Board theme, sound settings, analysis depth, and platform usernames stored securely in your browser's LocalStorage.
  • Chess & PGN Data: Portable Game Notation (PGN) text and move evaluations computed locally in your browser via Stockfish WebAssembly.
  • Zero Mandatory Accounts: No passwords or private profiles required to analyze unlimited chess games.
02

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Compute move evaluations, blunders, and accuracy scores via Stockfish 18 WASM.
  • Persist your custom board themes and sound settings across visits.
  • Fetch public games from Chess.com when requested.
03

3. Data Storage & Security Standards

All communication between your browser and our backend is encrypted via TLS 1.3. User sessions and access tokens are secured using cryptographic signatures and timed expiration.

Game analysis is executed entirely on your client device in a sandbox Web Worker, ensuring your games remain completely private.

04

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with vetted industry-leading providers for essential infrastructure:

  • Payment Processors: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
  • Google Identity Services: Optional single-sign-on OAuth authentication.
  • Lichess & Chess.com Public APIs: Optional read-only import of user's public games.
05

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your physical location, SabioChess extends the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Export all saved games in PGN format at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request permanent deletion of your account and associated game records.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your profile information, email, or connected platform usernames directly.
