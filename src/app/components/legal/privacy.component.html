TERMS OF SERVICE
01

1. What We Collect

SabioChess collects almost no data at all. There are no accounts, no emails, no passwords, no profiles, and no tracking.

  • Your preferences only: Board theme, sound pack, volume, analysis depth, dark/light mode, auto-evaluation toggle, and platform usernames (Chess.com / Lichess) are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • No game storage: PGN data you paste or import is processed in-memory only. It is never stored, uploaded, or retained after you close the tab.
  • No analytics or tracking: SabioChess does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, or any fingerprinting technology.
02

2. Local Storage & Settings

The only data persisted between sessions is your settings configuration, stored under the key sabiochess_settings_v2 in your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • App theme (dark / light)
  • Board theme selection
  • Sound pack choice and volume level
  • Analysis depth (10–22 plies)
  • Auto-evaluation and auto-queen promotion toggles
  • Chess.com and Lichess usernames (for game fetch convenience)

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data or using the "Reset to Defaults" button in the Settings tab.

03

3. Third-Party APIs

When you choose to import games by entering a Chess.com or Lichess username, SabioChess makes a direct read-only request to those platforms' public APIs:

  • Chess.com Public API: Fetches publicly available game archives for the provided username. No authentication or Chess.com account is required.
  • Lichess Public API: Fetches publicly available PGN game exports for the provided username. No authentication or Lichess account is required.

These requests are made from your browser directly to Chess.com and Lichess. SabioChess does not proxy, store, or log any game data retrieved from these APIs. Your username is used only to form the API request URL and is not transmitted to any SabioChess server.

By using the game import feature, you acknowledge that the data is subject to Chess.com's Privacy Policy and Lichess's Privacy Policy respectively.

04

4. Local Engine & Analysis Privacy

All chess analysis — move evaluation, CAPS score calculation, blunder detection, and opening book lookups — runs entirely inside your browser using Stockfish 18 compiled to WebAssembly (WASM).

Engine computation runs in an isolated Web Worker thread on your own device. No moves, positions, FEN strings, or PGN data are sent to any external server at any point during analysis.

Opening book lookups use locally bundled data. No external API calls are made for opening theory data.

05

5. Your Control

Because SabioChess stores nothing on any server, you are always in complete control of your data:

  • Clear settings: Use "Reset to Defaults" in the Settings tab, or clear your browser's site data for sabiochess.
  • No account to delete: There is no account, profile, or server-side record associated with your use of SabioChess. Nothing to request deletion of.
  • No opt-in required: No cookies, no consent banners, no marketing lists. You simply open the app and use it.

If you have any privacy concerns or questions, please reach out via our support channel.
